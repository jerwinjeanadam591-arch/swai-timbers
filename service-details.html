Frequently Asked Questions

  • What information should I include in an enquiry?

    Tell us the timber dimensions and quantities, any treatment requirements, and your delivery destination.

  • Can I ask about timber treatment?

    Yes. Include the intended use of the timber and the treatment you have in mind so we can discuss your requirements.

  • Can you help plan transportation?

    Share your destination, preferred timing and any site access details to help us understand the logistics required.

  • How is pricing determined?

    Pricing depends on the timber, quantities, treatment needs and delivery requirements. Contact us with your details to request a quote.
