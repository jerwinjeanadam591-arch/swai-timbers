Planning a Timber Order: Sawing, Treatment and Delivery

Start with the timber dimensions and quantities you need. Consider the conditions and purpose of the finished timber when discussing treatment, then share the delivery destination and any access or timing requirements.

Clear order details help the team understand what you need and discuss the next steps.

For a useful enquiry, include your timber specifications, treatment needs and delivery information.
